---
title: "Frames"
description: "A border and caption around an image or embed."
---

Frames give a screenshot a defined edge, which matters on a white background where the image would otherwise bleed into the page.

<Frame caption="A framed image">
  <img src="/images/preview.svg" alt="Example" />
</Frame>

```mdx
<Frame caption="A framed image">
  <img src="/images/preview.svg" alt="Example" />
</Frame>
```

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `caption` | `string` | — | Caption shown under the frame. |
