---
title: "Cards"
description: "Linkable cards with an icon, for navigation and grouping."
---

A card with an `href` becomes clickable in full. Wrap several in `<CardGroup>` (or `<Columns>`) for a grid.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="With a link" href="/quickstart" icon="rocket">
    Cards take an optional `href` and become clickable.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Without one" icon="book-open">
    Plain cards work too, for grouping related notes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

```mdx
<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="With a link" href="/quickstart" icon="rocket">
    Cards take an optional `href` and become clickable.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Without one" icon="book-open">
    Plain cards work too, for grouping related notes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
```

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `title` | `string` | — | Card heading. |
| `icon` | `string \| ReactNode` | — | Lucide name, or your own node. |
| `href` | `string` | — | Makes the whole card a link. |
| `cols` | `number` | `2` | On `<CardGroup>`: columns in the grid. |
