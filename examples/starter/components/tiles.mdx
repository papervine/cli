---
title: "Tiles"
description: "Cards that lead with a preview image."
---

Tiles pair with `<Columns>` for a visual grid — a gallery of templates or examples.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Tile href="/quickstart" title="Get started" description="Fork the repo and edit docs.json">
    <img src="/images/preview.svg" alt="Preview" />
  </Tile>
  <Tile title="No link" description="Tiles work without an href too">
    <img src="/images/preview.svg" alt="Preview" />
  </Tile>
</Columns>

```mdx
<Columns cols={2}>
  <Tile href="/quickstart" title="Get started" description="Fork the repo and edit docs.json">
    <img src="/images/preview.svg" alt="Preview" />
  </Tile>
  <Tile title="No link" description="Tiles work without an href too">
    <img src="/images/preview.svg" alt="Preview" />
  </Tile>
</Columns>
```

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `title` | `string` | — | Tile heading. |
| `description` | `string` | — | Supporting line under the title. |
| `href` | `string` | — | Makes the whole tile a link. |
